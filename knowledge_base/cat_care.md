# Adult Cat Care Routines

## Feeding schedule

Adult cats do best on two to four small meals per day rather than one large meal. Cats are
natural grazers whose wild feeding pattern is many small kills, so splitting food across
morning and evening at minimum reduces vomiting and overeating. A common pattern is a meal
around 07:00 and a second around 18:00.

Wet food should not be left out for more than about 4 hours at room temperature. If you feed
wet food before leaving for work, plan to remove the bowl rather than leaving it down all day.

## Water

Cats have a naturally low thirst drive and are prone to urinary tract problems and chronic
kidney disease when chronically underhydrated. Refill and rinse water bowls daily, and keep
water bowls away from food bowls, which many cats prefer. A daily water refill task around
09:00 is a reasonable default.

## Litter tray

Scoop litter trays at least once per day, ideally twice. Fully change the litter and wash the
tray weekly. The general rule is one tray per cat plus one spare. Cats will refuse a dirty
tray and toilet elsewhere in the house, so daily scooping is not optional maintenance.

Sudden changes in litter tray habits are an early warning sign of urinary blockage, which is
a medical emergency in male cats. Note any change and contact a vet.

## Play and enrichment

Indoor cats need two to three short interactive play sessions per day, roughly 10 to 15
minutes each, using wand toys that mimic prey movement. Cats are crepuscular, meaning they are
most active at dawn and dusk, so play sessions land best in the early morning and again in the
evening around 19:00 to 21:00. An evening play session followed by a meal mimics the natural
hunt-eat-groom-sleep cycle and reduces night-time waking.

## Grooming

Short-haired cats need brushing about once a week. Long-haired cats such as Persians and Maine
Coons need daily brushing to prevent painful matting. Most cats do not need bathing.

## Claws

Provide scratching posts rather than trimming claws aggressively. If trimming, every 2 to 3
weeks is typical, taking only the sharp translucent tip and avoiding the pink quick.
