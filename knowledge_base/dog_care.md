# Adult Dog Care Routines

## Daily exercise needs

Most adult dogs need 30 to 120 minutes of exercise per day, split across at least two
outings. High-energy working breeds such as border collies, huskies, and retrievers sit at
the top of that range and benefit from being split into a longer morning walk and a shorter
evening walk. Low-energy and brachycephalic breeds such as bulldogs and pugs sit at the
bottom of the range and should not be walked hard in heat.

A useful default for a healthy adult dog of unknown breed is two walks per day: roughly 30
minutes in the morning and 20 to 30 minutes in the evening.

## Feeding schedule

Adult dogs are typically fed twice daily, about 10 to 12 hours apart. Morning feeding
between 07:00 and 08:00 and evening feeding between 17:00 and 19:00 is a common pattern
that fits a standard work day. Keep feeding times consistent day to day, because dogs
regulate their digestion and toileting around a predictable schedule.

Do not exercise a dog vigorously in the hour immediately after a large meal. In deep-chested
breeds this is associated with bloat, which is a medical emergency. Schedule walks either
before a meal or at least 60 minutes after it.

## Toileting

An adult dog needs a toilet break roughly every 6 to 8 hours, and most need one first thing
in the morning and one last thing at night. A short garden or block trip late in the evening,
around 22:00, reduces overnight accidents.

## Enrichment and training

Dogs need mental work, not only physical work. Ten to fifteen minutes of training, scent
games, or puzzle feeders per day meaningfully reduces destructive behaviour in under-stimulated
dogs. Short sessions are more effective than long ones; two 10-minute sessions beat one
20-minute session.

## Grooming

Short-coated dogs need brushing about once a week. Double-coated and long-coated dogs need
brushing three or more times per week, rising to daily during seasonal shedding. Nail trims
are typically needed every 3 to 4 weeks. Most dogs need bathing only every 4 to 8 weeks;
over-bathing strips coat oils and causes dry skin.

## Time-of-day guidance

In hot weather, walk dogs early in the morning and after sunset, avoiding 11:00 to 16:00 when
pavement can burn paw pads. Press the back of your hand to the pavement for seven seconds; if
you cannot hold it there, it is too hot for paws.
